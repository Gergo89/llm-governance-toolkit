## EU AI Act and the Digital Omnibus

- White & Case, *EU AI Omnibus enters into force, amending the AI Act* — entry
  into force 27 July 2026, published in the Official Journal 24 July 2026 as
  Regulation (EU) 2026/1744; amended deadlines of 2 December 2027 (Annex III)
  and 2 August 2028 (Annex I); safety-component clarification; AI literacy
  softened; new CSAM/NCII prohibitions; extended AI Office supervision.
  https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act
- Gibson Dunn, *EU AI Act Omnibus Agreement — Postponed High-Risk Deadlines and
  Other Key Changes* — deadline table including the December 2026 transitional
  period and the postponement of regulatory sandboxes to 2 August 2027.
  https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/
- Certivo, *EU AI Act August 2026: What Applies After the Digital Omnibus* —
  Article 50 obligations applying from 2 August 2026; 2 December 2026 grace

## Keeping this current

Re-verify at least quarterly, and immediately on any of these triggers: a new
Official Journal publication amending the AI Act; publication of harmonised
standards; a NIST AI RMF 2.0 draft; a new state AI statute in a jurisdiction
you operate in.

Where a statement in this repository cannot be traced to a source here, treat
it as the authors' interpretation rather than as established fact.
